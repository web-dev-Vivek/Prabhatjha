Deep-Dive by Functional Area

Trade capture is the starting point of the ETRM lifecycle. A trade entered incorrectly creates downstream problems in Position → Valuation → P&L → Risk → Settlement → Reporting. My focus: not just whether a trade exists in the system, but whether it has been captured with the correct business attributes.

  • Counterparty & Portfolio
  • Commodity & Product
  • Buy / Sell Direction
  • Trade Date & Delivery Period
  • Quantity & Price
  • Currency & Location
  • Settlement Terms
  • Broker / Exchange

Market curves are critical because they provide the data required for valuation and risk calculations. A trade can be captured perfectly but still produce incorrect valuation if it references the wrong curve. I understand the full chain: Market → Curve → Index → Price Source → Tenor → Delivery Period → Valuation.

  • Power & Gas Curves
  • FX & Interest Rates
  • Price Source Validation
  • Forward Data Handling
  • Historical Price Loading
  • Curve-to-Trade Mapping
  • Missing Data Investigation
  • FMT Automated Imports

Valuation connects trading positions with market data, and P&L is one of the most important outputs of an ETRM system. I've worked on trade estimation, settlement calculations, and price & FX data imports. I investigate valuation issues across: Trade → Market Data → Curve → Pricing → Valuation.

  • Trade Quantity & Price
  • Forward Curve Reference
  • FX Impact Analysis
  • Delivery Period Mapping
  • Realized vs Unrealized P&L
  • Portfolio-Level Results
  • Cashflow Reporting Alignment
  • Back Office System Alignment

Settlement is where the commercial terms of a trade are translated into financial outcomes. Reconciliation ensures the numbers agree across systems. I've worked with JRA records, back-office dispute management, and SQL-level data investigation to identify where breaks occur.

  • Trade Estimation
  • Settlement Calculations
  • Settlement Validation
  • JRA Reconciliation
  • Back Office Dispute Management
  • FX Data Validation
  • Settlement-Related Testing
  • Root Cause Investigation (SQL)

ETRM reporting is not just about displaying numbers — it needs to answer business questions. My approach: Understand the business question → Understand the source data → Validate the calculation → Validate the output.

  • Trading Reports (Trades, Volumes, Prices)
  • Risk Reports (Exposure, Valuation)
  • P&L Reports (Realized & Unrealized)
  • Settlement Reports
  • Operational Reports (Exceptions, Failures)
  • Cashflow Reporting
  • Cross-System Data Alignment
  • ETRM Output Validation

Good ETRM testing verifies not just "Does the screen work?" but "Does the complete business process work?" Production support has taught me that ETRM issues are rarely isolated — a settlement incident may originate from trade configuration, reference data, or integration.

  • SIT & UAT
  • Sanity & Functional Testing
  • API Testing (SoapUI)
  • Integration Testing
  • Data Validation
  • SQL Root Cause Analysis
  • Incident Investigation
  • End-to-End Process Validation
